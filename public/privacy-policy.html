Privacy Policy for ValuVid

Last updated: November 10, 2026 • Effective Date: November 10, 2026

Mokarstech ("we", "us", or "our") operates the ValuVid mobile application and services. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service, and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, upload videos, engage with content, or participate in sponsored micro-bounties on ValuVid, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Name, username, email address, password hash, and profile avatar.
  • User-Generated Content: Videos, captions, audio selections, comments, and hashtags you create or upload.
  • Device & Usage Data: Device model, operating system version, app usage statistics, and engagement interactions (likes, shares, watch retention).
  • Payment & Wallet Information: Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash) or bank account details required to process tips, micro-bounties, and payouts. Payment processing is handled securely via Paystack; we never store your full card numbers on our servers.

2. Device Permissions We Request

To deliver creator features, ValuVid requests the following permissions on your Android device:

  • Camera: To record vertical and horizontal videos inside the studio.
  • Microphone: To record audio while filming content.
  • Storage / Media Photos & Videos: To allow you to pick and upload videos from your device gallery and save downloads.
  • Internet & Network State: To sync video feeds, stream media, and process wallet transactions.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the ValuVid video platform.
  • To process creator tips, brand-sponsored challenge prize payouts, and wallet cashouts.
  • To personalize content feeds and calculate trending/community creation rankings.
  • To prevent fraudulent activity, spam, and unauthorized account access.
  • To comply with legal obligations and Google Play Developer Program policies.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using cloud infrastructure provided by Supabase with industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Video media is hosted via verified content delivery endpoints.

5. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party providers who adhere to strict data security practices:

  • Paystack: PCI-DSS certified payment gateway for processing mobile money and card deposits/escrow payouts.
  • Supabase: Backend authentication, PostgreSQL database, and cloud storage.
  • Google Play Services: Core platform support and application distribution.

6. Account & Data Deletion (User Rights)

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data at any time:

  • Video Deletion: You can delete any of your uploaded videos directly inside the app under your Profile tab. Deleted videos are immediately and permanently removed from public feeds and database queries.
  • Account Deletion: You can request full deletion of your account and associated personal data by navigating to Settings > Delete Account or by contacting our Data Protection Officer at support@valuvid.app. Requests are completed within 7 business days.

7. Children's Privacy

ValuVid is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
